---
question: How do I report a shared document?
section: sharing
order: 10
---

Select **Report** at the top of the document, where the owner has Share, then choose a reason and add details if you like.

You need to be signed in. Without an account, write to [inbox@pencilcase.app](mailto:inbox@pencilcase.app) with the link and what you consider illegal or wrong. The owner is not told who reported the document.
