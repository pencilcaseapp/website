---
question: How do I share a document with a link?
section: sharing
order: 1
related:
  - do-others-need-an-account
---

Open the document, select **Share**, turn the switch on and copy the link. Anyone with the link can then read the document.

1. Open the document and select **Share** at the top.
2. Turn on the switch.
3. Choose whether people with the link can view or edit.
4. Select **Copy link** and send it to whoever you like.

A new link always starts as view only, so you never hand out editing by accident.
