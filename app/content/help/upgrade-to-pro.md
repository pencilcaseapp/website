question: How do I upgrade to Pro?
section: subscription
order: 2
related:
  - who-handles-payment
  - ios-and-android-apps
---

Select **Buy** under [Pricing](/#pricing), or go to [Settings → Subscription](https://docs.pencilcase.app/upgrade) in your browser and select **Upgrade to Pro**. You pay on Creem’s checkout page and come straight back.

If you are not signed in yet, you sign in first, and Pro goes to that account.

You cannot upgrade in the iOS and Android apps: Pro is sold on our website only. Once you have it, it works in the apps too.
