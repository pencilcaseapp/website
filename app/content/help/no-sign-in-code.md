---
question: I did not get my sign-in code. What now?
section: account
order: 2
---

Check your spam folder and the spelling of the address, then [ask for a new code](https://docs.pencilcase.app/signin).

If it still does not arrive, write to [inbox@pencilcase.app](mailto:inbox@pencilcase.app) from that address and we will look into it.
