---
question: How do I cancel my subscription?
section: subscription
order: 5
related:
  - when-pro-ends
  - refunds
---

Go to **Settings → Subscription → Manage subscription** and cancel on the page that opens, or use the [cancel contract](/cancel-contract) form, which needs no sign-in.

The form e-mails you a confirmation with the time your cancellation reached us.

The first year is a fixed term: if you cancel during it, Pro keeps running until the year is over. After that you can cancel at any time with one month’s notice, and we refund the unused part of the year you paid for.
