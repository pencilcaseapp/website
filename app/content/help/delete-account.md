---
question: How do I delete my account?
section: account
order: 7
related:
  - deleted-data
  - cancel-subscription
---

Go to **Settings → Account**, choose **Delete account** in the danger zone and confirm. This works on pencilcase.app and in the apps.

You are signed out on all your devices, and your documents close for everybody you shared them with. If you have Pro, it is cancelled and runs until the end of the period you paid for. We send you an e-mail that confirms the deletion.

For 30 days you can change your mind: sign in with the same e-mail address and your account is back, with all your documents. After that we permanently delete your account, your documents and their images.
