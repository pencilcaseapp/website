---
question: How do I pin a document?
section: documents
order: 4
related:
  - finding-documents
  - delete-a-document
---

Open the menu of its row in the sidebar and choose **Pin**. It moves to **Pinned** at the top of the list. Choose **Unpin** in the same menu to put it back.

The document you pinned last comes first. Pins are yours alone. Pinning a shared document does not pin it for anybody else.

Each [folder](/help/finding-documents) shows the pinned documents it holds. A document you pin in **Shared with you** is pinned in **All Docs** too. Your own documents never show up in **Shared with you**, pinned or not.

Deleting a document takes its pin away, and so does removing a shared document from your list. A restored document comes back unpinned.
